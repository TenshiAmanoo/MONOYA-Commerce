BRAND DESCRIPTION & IDENTITY: MONOYA (物屋) — JAPAN ARCHIVE DIRECT
=====================================================================

1. BRAND OVERVIEW
MONOYA (物屋) is an authentic vault and curated purveyor delivering rare Japanese archival fashion, designer vintage, and subcultural artifacts directly from Japan. Sourcing straight from Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka collector networks, MONOYA eliminates speculative middlemen to bring authenticated Japanese design history directly to collectors, stylists, and enthusiasts worldwide.

2. THE NAME: MONOYA (物屋)
- Mono (物): "Object / Thing / Material Artifact" — celebrating physical craft, textile weight, and artisanal presence.
- Ya (屋): "House / Sanctuary / Master Establishment" — reflecting specialized expertise and provenance.
Combined meaning: "The House of Objects" — an archival institution dedicated to the preservation of Japanese design treasures.

3. THE TAGLINE: JAPAN ARCHIVE DIRECT
"Archive Direct" signifies unmediated, verified access to Tokyo's vintage vaults, runway deadstock, and cultural grails without intermediary markups.

4. THE EMBLEM & SYMBOLISM
- Stitched Crimson Badge: A rounded square with hand-dashed white perimeter stitching, honoring garment maker tags, sashiko visible mending, and master craftsmanship.
- Ivory Disc & Archive Cross (+): The central medical/preservation cross signifies clinical authentication, condition preservation, and garment life-extension.

5. SHORT SOCIAL BIO
"MONOYA (物屋) ・ JAPAN ARCHIVE DIRECT connects collectors worldwide to authenticated Japanese archival fashion, visionary designer grails, and rare subcultural artifacts directly from Japan."

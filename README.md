# MONOYA-Commerce

ABOUT MONOYA (物屋) — JAPAN ARCHIVE DIRECT

At MONOYA (物屋, "House of Objects"), we believe that visionary clothing is living design history. 

From the avant-garde revolution of 1980s Paris-Tokyo runways to the fertile underground of 1990s Harajuku and early 2000s artisan denim, Japan has cultivated some of the most influential garment design in modern history. Yet authenticating and procuring these rare pieces across borders has historically been fraught with fragmented listings, counterfeit risks, and opaque provenance.

MONOYA bridges this gap through a direct Tokyo-to-collector conduit. Every garment and object in our vault undergoes rigorous physical authentication, archival condition reporting, and preservation inspection before international dispatch.
